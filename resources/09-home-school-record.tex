\toolsection{The Existing Camp Artifact}

The final camp communication package was a personalized full-week recap book for each of the team's two assigned youth. One completed PDF contains five landscape pages for Days 1--5. The other contains four pages for Days 2--5 because the learner did not attend Day 1. Each attended day receives its own page; each page tells the day through five activity cards, a child-forward image, transcript-grounded thinking, why the learning matters, and a concise evidence limit \citep{campEvidence2026}.

Personalized email drafts introduce the attachment and invite families to review it with the child. The production archive confirms completed PDFs, review renders, sources, image manifests, and drafts. It does \emph{not} contain a parent-delivery receipt or a family reply. The accurate claim is therefore that the full-week packages were completed and prepared for family delivery, not that receipt or response was confirmed.

\toolsection{Prepare, Review, and Deliver}

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Verify attendance, chosen name, pronouns, family language, readers, and image permission.
	\item Assemble each day's memos, artifacts, recap, reviewed images, and activity sequence.
	\item Mark exact words, documented action, team evidence, interpretation, and unknowns before layout.
	\item Let the learner select, edit, translate, restrict, replace, or withhold the outgoing story.
	\item Export an accessible private copy and check every day, all five activity cards, attribution, and evidence limits.
	\item Use an approved delivery channel, record a receipt when available, and invite a low-burden response. Without a receipt, say \textit{prepared}, not \textit{sent}.
\end{enumerate}

\toolsection{Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS in Practice}

The Puzzle Plan carries the student memo, daily souvenir, team recap, activity evidence, and week-long storyline forward without flattening them into one adult judgment. EQUITAS asks who controls the family narrative, whose days or activities are missing, whether interpretation is labeled, and whether the family can return knowledge in an accessible way. Family knowledge is evidence, not an afterthought \citep{zacarian2015community}.

\reflectionbox{\textbf{Artifact and delivery proof:} the private production bundle contains two final PDFs---five pages for one full attendance week and four for a learner who began on Day 2---plus review renders, source records, image manifests, and two personalized email drafts. No parent-delivery receipt or family response was recovered; neither is claimed \citep{campEvidence2026}.}

\adaptationbox{Family communication can become surveillance or burden. Collect only what supports learning; follow privacy policy; use approved channels; and honor no-photo, no-message, translation, audio, paper, event-triggered, weekly, or no-formal-log preferences. This internal UofR portfolio includes the completed page under signed family consent; circulation beyond the approved educational audience requires separate review.}
